# Supplement day boundary — 6.5.1

Supplement checkmarks and last-taken labels now use a local 4 a.m. boundary. For example, a 2 a.m. Monday entry belongs to Sunday's supplement day. The checklist refreshes at 4 a.m. while active and on foreground return. The date calculation uses local calendar dates so daylight saving changes do not shift the boundary.

Existing history is preserved without reassigning old dates. Workout days, fatigue and exercise recovery still use midnight. The checklist and history views explain the supplement boundary.

100 automated tests pass, including 3:59/4:00, month/year changes and both daylight saving transitions. Real-phone rendering remains unverified.
