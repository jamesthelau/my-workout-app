# Version 6.7 — Swipe to repeat and complete

Swipe a set row from left to right across its non-input area.

- Set 1 repeats the most recently logged weight and reps from an earlier day for that exact exercise/variation.
- Later sets repeat the completed or filled-in row immediately above.
- The swipe saves the set immediately, starts the normal rest timer, and offers Undo. Finishing the planned sets follows the normal timer and pin cleanup.
- Existing weight/reps in the destination are replaced. Previous drop rounds and partial flags are not copied; destination drop rounds are retained.
- Missing history or an empty/invalid source does not save a set. Completed rows are protected. Vertical scrolling, reverse swipes, and canceled gestures do not complete sets.
- Swipe outside text fields, buttons, and drop-round controls to preserve normal editing.

Validation: 104 automated tests passed. Real-device Android gestures have not been verified in this environment.

Upload the updated files to the existing hosting location. Close and reopen the app to activate its refreshed offline cache. Existing local workout data is retained.
